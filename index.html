What Amazon's policy says

  • List prices are validated against recent sales history, recent featured offer prices and other retailers.
  • A list price is validated when the product is purchased on Amazon at that price. Only orders that match the featured offer price count.
  • The lookback period is 90 days.
  • A product needs at least 9 days of featured offer data to qualify for validation through Amazon sales history.
  • Our offer must be lower than the list price.

Source: Amazon Seller Assistant, list price validation.

Our playbook

  1. Raise List Price and Your Price to the test priceAn increase of 8% to 15% has worked as well as 30% to 60%.
  2. Hold it as the featured offer for 10 to 11 daysKeep the Buy Box and get at least one order at the raised price. It doesn't need to be near the end.
  3. Revert Your Price onlyKeep the List Price at the test price. Check the product page on day 1, 2 and 3 after the revert.
